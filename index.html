LAKESIDE DAYS
01 / RESTORE

Into
the pines.

Forest walks, lake air and a slower kind of wellness.

Leave an unhurried morning for a forest walk. Look for a lakeside retreat with a sauna or spa, then return to the water before sunset.
02 / ROAM

Follow
the trail.

A saddle, a forest path, and room to wander.

Arrange a guided ride with a local operator. Share your riding experience beforehand and ask for a forest route suited to your confidence.
03 / STAY

Sleep
under stars.

Canvas, comfort, and a front row seat to nature.

Choose a managed glamping site or forest lodge. Confirm heating, bathroom facilities and transfers before reserving; shoulder-season nights turn cool.
04 / CONNECT

Stories
over tea.

Kazakh hospitality. Local voices. A place at the table.

Visit the Botai-Burabay Museum and the Glade of Abylai Khan. Ask a local guide about the stories behind the stone, then make time for tea.
